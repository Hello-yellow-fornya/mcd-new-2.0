Online Claims Report

Brand guidelines, one page · v1.0 · 8 September 2026 · Sister brand to Motor Claims Department and Claims 247. Same claims, same handlers, a different door: this is the one where you report it online.

1. Wordmark

Online Claims Report

Online Claims Report
Online Claims Report
  • Inter Black, letter-spacing −4.5%, one line, never stacked. "Online" in green on paper, white and navy; the whole mark in one colour on green and in mono. Minimum 120px wide on screen. Clear space of one cap height all round.
  • No symbol, no strapline in the lockup, no outline, no shadow, never over photography.

  • Three highlights, used one at a time: the green chip on the H1 payoff; the green bar under one or two words in a heading; the mint underlay in body headings. Line spacing is measured from the top of a chip, never the glyphs.

4. Icons and illustration

  • Line icons: 1.9px navy stroke, round caps and joins, in a 36–44px mint circle. On navy the circle is green with a navy icon. Never solid glyphs, never two-tone.
  • Illustration is desktop only and optional: 4px navy line, mint and white fills, one green element. No characters, no wreckage, nothing AI-generated.

  • Pills everywhere, 999px radius; cards 14–20px radius with a hairline; the form is a white card with 10px fields. On mobile the online report is the green primary and the call is the mint secondary with the wait row beneath it — this is the one brand where online leads.

6. Voice, and the rules that already apply

  • Short, certain sentences. "You" more than "we". Sentence case. The catch in the open, once, in the site's one wording.
  • Name the mechanism: their insurer pays; nothing on your policy; one handler, start to finish; managed directly with the at-fault insurer.
  • The layout rulebook applies in full: fold-locked mobile heroes with one flexible gap; the last hero element on the fold; sizes grow before gaps; equal-height cards; the nav with the wordmark left and the call pill and burger right.
  • No exclamation marks, no slogans, no "week(s)", no "stress-free", no "navigating", no urgency, no naming insurers outside the landing pages.
  • No "Your insurer has a claims department" band — that line belongs to the other two brands. This one says what happens after you report it.
  • No pink, no photography in the hero, no solid icons, no second bright colour, no claim that is not on the substantiation list.
