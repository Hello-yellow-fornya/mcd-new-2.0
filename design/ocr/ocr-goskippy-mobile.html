Straight answers

What’s the catch?
We recover our costs from the at-fault driver’s insurer, which is why it costs you nothing. If fault can’t be established, you could be asked to cover the hire charges. So we tell you when we ring back whether your claim is one we’d take on.
Do I still have to tell my insurer?
Yes, that it happened. That’s a notification, not a claim. Nothing goes on your policy.
Will my premium go up?
Nothing goes through your policy, so there’s no claim on it. You still declare the incident at renewal, and how your insurer prices that is up to them. We won’t pretend otherwise.
Are you an insurer?
No. We don’t sell insurance. We make it pay.
