How it works

Someone hit you. Their insurer has to put it right — not yours. Most people don’t know that. We claim from their insurer instead.

  1. 1Tell us what happened. One call, or your reg. That’s your bit done.
  2. 2Your handler takes it on. One person, in the UK, deals with the other driver’s insurer. If they ring you, send them to us.
  3. 3We put you back in a car. Like-for-like, on your drive, while yours is repaired.
  4. 4They pay. Not you. No excess, nothing on your policy, your no-claims untouched.

Frequently asked questions

The catch, and everything else people ask.

What’s the catch?
We recover our costs from the at-fault driver’s insurer, which is why it costs you nothing. If they refuse to accept fault, we argue it for you. In the rare case that fault can’t be established, you could be asked to cover the hire charges — which is why we tell you on the first call whether your claim is one we’d take on.
Do I still have to speak to my insurer?
Only to let them know it happened. That’s a notification, not a claim. We handle everything else, including the call you’ll get from the other driver’s insurer.
Will my premium go up?
Nothing goes through your policy, so there’s no claim on it. You’ll still need to tell your insurer about the incident at renewal, and how they price that is up to them. We won’t pretend otherwise.
Are you an insurer?
No. We don’t sell insurance. We just make it pay.
