## What credit hire is

When you have a non-fault accident, you are entitled to a replacement vehicle for the period your car is off the road. Credit hire is the arrangement that makes that possible without upfront cost to you.

You take possession of the car. The hire company extends credit. We recover the hire cost from the at-fault driver’s insurer as part of your claim. Credit hire is well established in motor claims law and has been tested in the courts on many occasions.

<Callout lead="The short version">You get the car now. The hire is on credit. On a non-fault claim we recover the cost from the driver who hit you directly through their insurer, meaning you have nothing to pay.</Callout>

## What you sign, and what it means

You sign two documents:

- A credit hire agreement; this records that you are taking the vehicle on credit, with the cost to be recovered from the at-fault insurer. It is not a standard consumer credit agreement.
- A credit repair agreement, if we arrange the repair where the same structure applies to repair costs.

Your obligations are to cooperate with the claim, provide a statement of what happened, and attend a hearing if the matter reaches court and is not settled beforehand. In the large majority of cases, it does not.

## Who pays, and how

The at-fault driver’s insurer pays. We submit the hire costs, repair costs, and any other reasonable losses to them as part of the third party claim. They settle directly with us. You are not involved in that transaction.

If the at-fault insurer disputes the rate or duration of the hire, we deal with that dispute. It does not come back to you.

## Why it’s free to you

Our costs are recovered from the at-fault insurer. We do not charge you a fee, and you do not pay for the hire car. The legal costs of pursuing the claim are met by us, not by you.

This works because the at-fault insurer is liable for your losses. We confirm that on the first call, before anything is signed.
