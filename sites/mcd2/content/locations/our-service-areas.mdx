## Where we operate

Our core coverage runs across Greater London and into the Essex corridor, with a particular focus on east London, Ilford and Romford. These are the areas where our recovery network, approved repairers and local knowledge are strongest.

We provide two main services in these areas: non-fault accident management and vehicle recovery from the scene of an accident. Both can be arranged with a single call to our team.

## Accident management in your area

Non-fault accident management involves taking over the claim against the at-fault driver’s insurer on your behalf. We handle the correspondence, the liability investigation and the recovery of your losses, and we arrange a replacement vehicle while yours is off the road.

Much of this work is handled remotely, which means that if you are based in or around London and Essex, we can manage your claim efficiently without unnecessary delays. You will have a named handler throughout and a clear picture of where your claim stands at each stage.

<Callout lead="What accident management covers">We pursue the at-fault driver’s insurer for your repair costs, a like-for-like replacement vehicle for the full period you are without your car, recovery and storage costs, and any reasonable out-of-pocket losses caused by the accident. Nothing goes through your own policy.</Callout>

To understand more about how the process works from start to finish, see [how accident management works](/how-accident-management-works/).

## Vehicle recovery

If your vehicle cannot be driven away from the scene of an accident, we arrange recovery to an approved repairer within our network. This is a local operation and our ability to respond quickly depends on where you are.

Within London, Essex, Ilford and Romford, we arrange recovery as soon as we can after your call. The vehicle is taken directly to a repairer we have vetted and worked with, so you do not need to source one yourself.

### What we arrange

- Recovery from the scene to an approved repairer
- Storage of the vehicle if repairs cannot begin immediately
- Delivery of a like-for-like replacement vehicle to your address
- Collection of the replacement when the claim concludes

For total loss situations, see [car written off, not my fault](/car-written-off-not-my-fault/).

## London

Greater London is our primary operating area. We handle a high volume of non-fault claims across the capital and have approved repairers positioned to cover the majority of London postcodes. Whether you are in east London, central London or further into the north-east boroughs, our team can take your call and get things moving.

London roads carry some of the highest accident volumes in the country. A large proportion of those accidents involve a clearly at-fault driver, which means non-fault accident management is a practical and widely-used route for London drivers.

## Essex, Ilford and Romford

Our coverage extends out of London into Essex, with Ilford and Romford being the areas where our local presence is strongest. Both sit at the boundary of Greater London and Essex and fall within our standard coverage zone for both accident management and vehicle recovery.

## Not sure if we cover you?

If your location is not one of the areas listed above, it is worth getting in touch before assuming we cannot help. Accident management can often be handled wherever you are in England and Wales, because the claim process runs between us and the at-fault driver’s insurer rather than requiring us to be on the ground near you.

What is location-dependent is vehicle recovery. If you need a vehicle recovered from the scene or delivered to an address outside our standard area, call us and we will tell you immediately whether we can cover it.
