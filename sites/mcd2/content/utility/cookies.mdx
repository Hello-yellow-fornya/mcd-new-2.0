Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, and to give the site owner information about how the site is used.

This site uses two kinds of cookie: essential cookies, which we set without asking because the site does not work without them, and optional cookies, which we set only if you accept them.

## Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the site to function. They keep the site secure, keep it working across pages, and remember the cookie choice you made so we do not ask you again on every visit. You cannot turn them off through this site.

## Optional cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics and Google Ads to measure how the site is used, which pages people visit, and which enquiries come from which source. This helps us understand what is useful and improve the site.

Nothing in this group is set until you accept it. If you reject optional cookies, none of these cookies are placed on your device and no measurement data is collected.

## Cookies set by other organisations

Where we use Google Analytics and Google Ads, those cookies are set by Google, and Google processes the data as described in its own privacy notice. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles about you outside the measurement described above.

## Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though blocking essential cookies may stop parts of this site working.

## More information

For how we handle your personal information more generally, see our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/). If you have a question about cookies on this site, call us on 0208 988 9508.
