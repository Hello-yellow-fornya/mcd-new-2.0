## Non-fault accident management in London

London has some of the highest accident rates in the UK. Incidents range from low-speed car park collisions in residential boroughs to more complex multi-vehicle accidents on major roads. The claim process is the same wherever you are.

You get one named handler who manages your case from the first call to settlement. They notify the at-fault insurer, arrange the replacement car, and oversee the repair. You do not deal with the other driver’s insurer.
