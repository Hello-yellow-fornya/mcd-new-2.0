## What an accident management company does

An accident management company steps in after a non-fault road accident and manages the claim process on your behalf. Instead of going through your own insurer and paying an excess, we pursue the at-fault driver’s insurer directly for everything you are entitled to.

We handle the paperwork, the correspondence, the liability investigation and the logistics. From the replacement car to the approved repair, you have a named handler, a single point of contact, and a clear picture of where your claim stands at every stage.

### What we arrange on your behalf

- A like-for-like replacement vehicle delivered to your address
- Repairs carried out at an approved bodyshop
- Recovery of your vehicle from the scene if needed
- All correspondence with the at-fault driver’s insurer
- Evidence gathering if liability is disputed
- Settlement of your out-of-pocket losses

## Who we work for

We work for you, not for your insurer. That distinction matters more than it might seem.

When you make a claim through your own insurer, they appoint their own people to process it. Their interest is in settling the matter at the lowest cost to them. When you come to us, our job is to recover everything you are entitled to from the person who caused the accident.

<Callout lead="The claims department on your side">Your insurer has a claims team. It is there to process your claim efficiently for your insurer. We exist to recover every penny you are owed from the driver who caused the accident, without it touching your own policy.</Callout>

## How we differ from your insurer

The two routes look similar from the outside. The difference is in who pays, who benefits, and what happens to your policy.

## What we recover for you

On a straightforward non-fault claim, the at-fault driver’s insurer is liable for the following losses. We pursue each of them on your behalf.

- The full cost of repairing your vehicle at an approved repairer
- A like-for-like replacement vehicle for the entire period your car is off the road
- Recovery and storage costs if your vehicle had to be towed from the scene
- A total loss settlement at market value if your car is written off
- Reasonable out-of-pocket expenses caused directly by the accident, subject to receipts

For more on the total loss route, see [car written off, not my fault](/car-written-off-not-my-fault/).

## The catch

This route works in specific circumstances. If those circumstances do not apply, we will tell you clearly on the first call, before you have committed to anything.

<Callout lead="When it works">The non-fault route is available when another driver was clearly at fault and holds valid motor insurance. If fault is genuinely unclear, or if the other driver was uninsured or left the scene, a different process applies. We check this on the first call and advise you on the right route before you sign anything.</Callout>
